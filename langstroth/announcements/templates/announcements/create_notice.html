Create a Notice

A notice announces a hazard or security issue that is not (yet) a service outage — for example a CVE affecting instances, or a heatwave that may affect data centre cooling. It carries a severity and can be updated as the situation evolves; end it when the hazard is stood down. Unlike an outage, a planned end is always optional and no initial update is required.
